After 22 Years

Poem Version

22 Years of Waiting

Two decades of longing, a path full of pain,
But the Gunners of London are champions again.
Twenty-two years of waiting, the drought is now gone,
Under May's golden skies, a new era has begun.

Arteta's Redemption

Three times they were runners-up, chasing the crown,
But Mikel Arteta refused to let the team down.
With defensive perfection and hearts full of fire,
They conquered the league and fulfilled their desire.

Red and White Glory

The Emirates is roaring, the flares light the street,
As the champions of England finally meet.
The trophy is lifted, the history is made,
A beautiful triumph that will never fade.
